# Rebel Bento Linktree — Design Brainstorm

## Project Context
A rebellious, anti-corporate, graffiti-styled Bento linktree. Video cards that flip (video front, link back). Corner grab-and-drag to resize. Everything stays in iFrames — never leave the platform. No data collection. Privacy-first. Take back from big tech.

## Idea 1: "Concrete Jungle" — Raw Urban Brutalism

**Design Movement:** Neo-Brutalist meets Street Art. Think abandoned warehouse walls covered in wheat-paste posters and spray paint, combined with raw concrete textures and exposed structural elements.

**Core Principles:**
1. Raw authenticity — nothing polished, everything feels hand-made and real
2. Aggressive asymmetry — layouts that feel like they were slapped onto a wall, not designed in Figma
3. High contrast rebellion — neon spray paint colors screaming against dark concrete
4. Anti-corporate transparency — everything is visible, nothing is hidden behind corporate UX patterns

**Color Philosophy:** Deep charcoal concrete (#1a1a1a) as the base — the wall. Electric neon accents: toxic green (#39FF14), hot magenta (#FF006E), electric cyan (#00F5FF), warning yellow (#FFE600). These are spray paint colors — they glow against the dark. White (#F0F0F0) for readable text, but distressed.

**Layout Paradigm:** Scattered Bento — cards placed at slight rotations (1-3deg), overlapping edges, like posters on a wall. No perfect grid. Cards have torn-edge borders and drip effects. The overall composition feels like a curated chaos wall.

**Signature Elements:**
- Dripping paint effects on card borders and text
- Stencil-cut typography that looks spray-painted
- Glitch/static noise texture overlays on hover

**Interaction Philosophy:** Aggressive and tactile. Cards shake slightly on hover like they're being ripped off the wall. Flip animations are fast and punchy with a slight overshoot. Drag handles look like duct tape corners.

**Animation:** Cards enter with a slam effect (scale from 1.2 to 1 with slight bounce). Paint drip animations on borders. Glitch flicker on text hover. Resize handles pulse with a neon glow.

**Typography System:** 
- Display: "Permanent Marker" (Google Fonts) — hand-written graffiti feel
- Body: "Space Mono" — monospace, hacker aesthetic, readable
- Accents: "Bungee Shade" for big rebellious headers

## Idea 2: "Digital Anarchist Zine" — Punk Collage Aesthetic

**Design Movement:** Punk Zine / Anti-Design. Inspired by 1970s-80s punk rock zines, ransom note typography, photocopied textures, and the DIY ethos of "anyone can make this."

**Core Principles:**
1. Deliberate imperfection — misaligned elements, mixed media, cut-and-paste feel
2. Information as weapon — bold statements, manifesto-style copy
3. Layered chaos — overlapping elements, mixed textures, collage composition
4. DIY empowerment — the aesthetic says "you don't need a corporation to exist online"

**Color Philosophy:** Newsprint off-white (#F5F0E8) as the base — like a photocopied zine page. Black (#0D0D0D) for heavy ink. Accent with anarchist red (#E63946), safety orange (#FF6B35), and electric violet (#7B2FBE). Colors are applied in blocks and splatters, never gradients.

**Layout Paradigm:** Collage Grid — cards overlap and layer like cut-out magazine pieces glued onto a page. Some cards are "taped" down with visible tape strips. The grid is intentionally broken — some cards span unexpected areas, others are tiny and tucked into corners.

**Signature Elements:**
- Scotch tape and safety pin decorations on card corners
- Ransom-note mixed typography (different fonts per word)
- Photocopied/halftone texture overlay on images and videos

**Interaction Philosophy:** Tactile and analog. Cards feel like they're being peeled off the page on hover. Flip animations mimic turning a zine page. Resize feels like tearing paper to a new size.

**Animation:** Cards enter with a stamp/press effect. Hover causes a slight lift with paper shadow. Tape strips wiggle on interaction. Text scrambles briefly before settling.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Rubik Mono One" — bold, blocky, protest-sign energy
- Body: "Courier Prime" — typewriter feel, raw and unpolished
- Accents: "Creepster" or hand-drawn style for rebellious callouts

## Idea 3: "Neon Vandal" — Cyberpunk Graffiti Noir

**Design Movement:** Cyberpunk Noir meets Street Art. Dark alleyway aesthetics with neon graffiti tags, rain-slicked surfaces, and the feeling of a digital underground. Think Blade Runner meets Banksy.

**Core Principles:**
1. Dark rebellion — the interface lives in the shadows, away from corporate light
2. Neon defiance — bright tags and glows that cut through the darkness like graffiti on a night wall
3. Encrypted privacy — visual cues that reinforce "your data stays yours"
4. Underground network — the feeling of being part of something hidden and powerful

**Color Philosophy:** Near-black base (#0a0a0f) — the dark alley wall. Neon graffiti colors that glow: electric pink (#FF2D7B), acid green (#00FF9F), laser blue (#00D4FF), amber warning (#FFB800). These colors have glow effects (box-shadow, text-shadow) to simulate neon light bleeding onto wet surfaces. Subtle purple (#2a1a3e) for depth layers.

**Layout Paradigm:** Stacked Depth Bento — cards float at different z-levels with parallax-like depth. The layout uses a loose grid but cards cast neon-edged shadows. Background has subtle animated rain/static. Cards feel like screens mounted on a dark wall.

**Signature Elements:**
- Neon glow borders that pulse and flicker like real neon signs
- Graffiti tag watermarks behind cards
- Scan-line / CRT monitor effect overlay

**Interaction Philosophy:** Electric and alive. Cards spark with neon on hover. Flip animations have a holographic glitch mid-transition. Drag resize leaves temporary neon trails. Everything feels like interacting with a hacked terminal.

**Animation:** Cards materialize with a digital glitch-in effect (horizontal slice reveal). Neon borders pulse rhythmically. Background has slow-moving particle/rain effect. Text types itself in on first load. Resize corners glow brighter when grabbed.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Russo One" — bold, angular, cyberpunk energy
- Body: "JetBrains Mono" — clean monospace, hacker terminal feel
- Accents: "Orbitron" for data labels and small caps

## Selected Approach: Idea 3 — "Neon Vandal" (Cyberpunk Graffiti Noir)

This approach best captures the rebellious, anti-corporate, graffiti aesthetic while providing the most visually striking dark interface. The neon-on-dark palette naturally communicates "underground" and "private." The cyberpunk elements reinforce the tech-savvy, privacy-first message. The depth-based layout creates the most engaging Bento experience with the video cards.
